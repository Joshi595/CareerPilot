# Frequently asked questions

**Help says my AI is "not installed".**
Install it from the vendor (Claude: claude.com/download; ChatGPT/Codex: developers.openai.com/codex; Google:
antigravity.google), then press *Check again* or *Test*. To sign in, press *Sign in*: a window opens where you log in once.

**"Your AI plan's usage limit is reached."**
Plans have a limit per few hours. Wait until it resets, or choose *Economy* in Settings → AI (uses less of your plan).

**The search finds nothing / a source shows a problem.**
Settings → *Job search* → *Job sources* shows what each source returned. Job sites change often; a source that fails a few days in a row
probably changed its page. Copy a problem report (Help) and open an issue.

**Where do the "career sites" come from?**
CareerPilot learns them by itself. When a posting links to a company's own careers page, when it reaches one while
applying, or when a company it has seen uses a common hiring system, that site goes on your list. From then on it is
searched directly, at most once a day. Settings → *Career sites* shows the list and where each site came from.
You can also paste a careers link there yourself.

**How do I stop it scanning one company's site?**
Settings → *Career sites*, then switch that site off. It stays off, even if the site is found again. A site that fails
3 times in a row pauses itself for a week.

**What is "Less relevant" on the Board?**
Every posting the search found but didn't put on your board, each with the reason: a weak match, another job type,
a word or company you hid, or off-topic. Nothing is thrown away. *Move to my board* (or ⚡, or *Apply*) brings one back
for good. Use the chips above the list to see one reason at a time.


**What does the "Ask CareerPilot" box do?**
At the top of Settings and Profile, type what you want in your own words: "Also search in Munich", "Make my letters
less formal", "My earliest start is 1 January", "Add Docker to my skills". It shows what would change in your settings,
CV instructions, letter instructions, application details or CV, each with a tick box, and saves only what you tick
when you press *Apply*. It can't change your email or passwords: those only change on their own pages.

**How do I tell CareerPilot how to write my CV and letters?**
Profile › *Writing* has your CV instructions and letter instructions, one rule per line. Every application follows
them. You can also add a rule while reviewing an application ("Always do something?"), or ask in the Ask box.

**A job posting won't load ("needs a login or JavaScript").**
Copy the whole posting text from your browser and paste it into *Apply to a job*.

**Why did it remove a line from my CV?**
Each change on the review page says why. Press *Keep my original* to undo it. Nothing is ever added that isn't in your profile.

**A check says "numbers not in your profile".**
The text contains a number your profile doesn't have. That's the safety net against invented facts: fix the line, or add
the fact to your profile if it's true.


**How do I update?**
Help → *Check for updates*. Your data is never touched by an update.

**How do I move to a new computer?**
Help → *Download a backup* on the old one. Install CareerPilot on the new one, then unzip the backup into the new data
folder (Help → *Open folder*). Passwords are not in the backup; enter them again in Settings.
