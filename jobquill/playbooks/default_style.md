# Writing style

Goal: text a hiring manager reads as written by a capable person, not generated.

## Ground rules
- Facts only. Every claim, tool and number comes from the profile. Never invent metrics, tools, team sizes or outcomes.
- Specific beats general: "cut processing time from 6 hours to 40 minutes", not "significantly improved performance".
- Plain verbs: built, wrote, designed, measured, fixed, ran, tested, reduced, cared for, taught.
- Mix sentence lengths. Avoid stacking three adjectives or three-item lists in every sentence.
- No em-dashes. Use a full stop, comma or brackets.

## Cover letter
- One page, about 350 words, 4-5 paragraphs. No bullet lists.
- Open with the role and one concrete reason for this employer. Never "I am writing to apply".
- Two pieces of evidence matched to the posting's top requirements, each with a number or concrete outcome.
- Frame it forward: which of their tasks the person can take on, with past work as the proof.
- Close with the earliest start date and, if relevant, work-permit status. Short thanks.
- Greeting to a named person when known, otherwise a neutral team greeting in the letter's language.
